=== Disclosure boundary and consent-linked disclosure

Consent declarations define what is permitted to be done with an asset or identity.
In some implementations, consent declarations MAY also influence what information is disclosed to a given party.

Disclosure behavior is external to the consent assertion and is evaluated at time of use.
Consent declarations MAY serve as inputs to such evaluation but do not define it.

NOTE: Selective disclosure mechanisms may be implemented by external identity or privacy-preserving frameworks.
This specification does not define such mechanisms and does not require any particular technology.
Consent declarations defined herein are intended to be compatible with such systems where implementations choose to use them.

This specification does not define how disclosure decisions are evaluated, how disclosure policies are structured, or how selective disclosure is implemented or enforced.
The presence of a consent assertion does not imply that all associated identity or metadata must be disclosed in all contexts.
